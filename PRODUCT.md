# BrewGo Coffee

## Register

product

## Users

Customers ordering on a phone during a morning commute or at a desktop before leaving work. They want to choose a nearby cafe, customize coffee or tea, add food, and collect their order quickly.

## Product Purpose

A working React and FastAPI application for ordering ahead. Success means a clear store choice, accurate order total, simulated payment, and a useful pickup confirmation. Sample stores are in US cities and prices are in US dollars.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, fast, consistent. Starbucks is the reference for familiar menu categories, customization, store selection, and pickup ordering. BrewGo has its own identity and content.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, glass panels, gradient text, repeated promotional cards, fake ratings, and invented rewards claims. Keep product photography separate from meaningful controls. Do not collect real card details.

## Design Principles

- Make the next ordering step clear.
- Show prices and customization costs before adding an item.
- Keep the pickup store visible throughout the purchase.
- Support guest checkout and an optional clearly labeled sample customer account.
- Confirm orders without contacting a payment provider.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA with readable contrast, keyboard controls, visible focus, semantic labels, screen reader feedback, reduced motion, and responsive layouts.
